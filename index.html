How Rent Increase Caps Work

1

Statutory Formulas

State laws like California AB 1482 link allowable rent hikes to regional Consumer Price Index inflation.

2

Ceiling Caps

Even in high-inflation years, absolute statutory caps (such as 10% in CA and OR) safeguard tenants.

3

Notice Deadlines

Landlords must provide written advance notice (typically 30 to 90 days) prior to lease renewals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum legal rent increase under California AB 1482?

Under California AB 1482, annual rent increases are capped at 5 percent plus regional CPI inflation or 10 percent, whichever is lower.

How much notice must a landlord give before increasing rent?

In most rent-controlled jurisdictions, landlords must provide written notice 30 days in advance for increases under 10 percent, and 90 days for higher increases.

What can tenants do if a proposed rent increase exceeds the legal cap?

Tenants can submit a written objection citing local rent control statutes, request an adjusted lease notice, or file a complaint with their local rent board.
